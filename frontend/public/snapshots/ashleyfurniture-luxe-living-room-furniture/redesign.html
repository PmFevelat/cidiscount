Modern Luxury Living Room Furniture FAQ

Ashley Luxe brings modern living room furniture that matches the quality and design standards of luxury pieces. Find answers below to questions about creating a modern luxury living room.

Modern living rooms prioritize warmth and longevity over temporary trends. Rounded edges, natural textures, and richer colors create spaces designed for actual living.

Modern living room essentials: 

  • Curved designs: Sofas and sectional sofas with soft lines make rooms feel more inviting 
  • Textured fabrics: Velvet, linen, and woven materials in seating and ottomans add visual interest 
  • Deep, rich colors: Deep greens, browns, and ochre replace pale neutrals 
  • Built to last: Quality construction in every piece means furniture worth keeping 
  • Mixed materials: Wood paired with metal accents in tables and storage pieces 
  • Smart function: Coffee tables with storage, ottomans that work multiple ways 

These trends point toward a more intentional approach to furnishing your space. Explore the Ashley Luxe collection for modern living room ideas.

Modern living room layouts work best when furniture floats away from walls, creating defined seating zones.

  • Anchor with your main piece: Arrange seating to face your fireplace, TV, or main window 
  • Add balance: Position chairs or a  loveseat opposite to complete the seating group 
  • Center your table: Keep it within easy reach of all seating 
  • Fill gaps thoughtfully: Add flexible pieces where needed without blocking flow 

Move pieces around until traffic flow feels natural and seating feels balanced. 

High-end living room furniture combines real comfort with lasting design. Our luxury living room collection delivers quality pieces built for how you live. 

Browse our collection: 

  • Sofas and sectional sofas : Quality construction for everyday comfort 
  • Coffee tables : Functional pieces that anchor your room 
  • Ottomans : Add flexibility for seating, storage, or footrests 
  • Accent chairs : Complementary seating that ties your room together 
  • Area rugs : Define your space and add visual warmth 
  • End Tables : Designed to work with your existing pieces 

Each piece combines quality materials with thoughtful function, building spaces ready for everyday life. If you’re looking for professional designers to help create the modern luxury living room of your dreams, contact the  Ashley Interior Design Services team .
